# Snoopy's Backyard AR Mural

An augmented reality street art mural featuring Snoopy and a giant lizard, powered by **MindAR**.

## 🎨 Mural Concepts & Features
* **Snoopy Sleeping:** Features floating "zZz's" and a dream bubble hovering in 3D digital space.
* **The Giant Lizard:** Animated shifting eyes tracking a butterfly, with a snapping tongue.
* **The Environment:** Shimmering, sparkling stars over a nighttime sky, with flowers and red *Amanita* mushrooms on the grass.

## 📱 How to View the AR
1. Scan the project QR code with your phone or iPad camera.
2. Open the webpage link (no app download required!).
3. Point your device's camera straight at the physical mural to watch it come to life.
